Hi! I've read the CBSE website. What would you like to know?
Where can I find the updated syllabus for Class 10 & 12?
The updated syllabus is located under the Curriculum tab in the main navigation menu. It contains the revised course structure for both Secondary (Class 10) and Senior Secondary (Class 12) levels.

Key Updates for this year:
• The theory exams will consist of 80 marks with a heavier emphasis on competency-based questions (up to 50%). [1]
• Internal assessments and practicals make up the remaining 20 marks, focusing on continuous evaluation. [2]
• Subjects like AI and Data Science have received new, expanded modules. [3]
Are there any changes to the practical exams?
Yes! The practical exams and internal assessments now carry a strict 20% weightage across most subjects.

Furthermore, schools are mandated to conduct these practicals under the supervision of external examiners appointed by the Board to ensure transparency. [4]
What about the passing criteria? Has that changed?
The passing criteria remains largely the same. Students must score a minimum of 33% overall to pass.

However, for subjects with both theory and practical components, you must secure 33% separately in both theory and practicals. [5]
When will the date sheet be released?
According to the latest circulars on this page, the tentative date for the release of the final board exam date sheet is typically in late December. Keep an eye on the "Latest @ CBSE" section for official announcements! [6]
